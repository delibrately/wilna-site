AquaKey: Exploiting the Randomness of the Underwater Visible Light Communication Channel for Key Extraction

Jingwen Wei, Lupeng Zhang, Zhang Jingchi, Lei Wang

Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies · 2025

DOI ↗ CCF A
English version

摘要

利用水下信道（UVLC）的自然随机随机性提取物理层对称密钥。通过环境自适应量化算法，在无预设密钥下实现安全通信，有效抵御窃听风险。
