content = "Li Zhongwei, Ph.D., is a faculty member in the Software College at Dalian University of Technology. He graduated in Mathematics from Dalian University of Technology in 2010, received an M.S. in Computer Science from Southern University and A&M College in 2012, and earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Arlington in 2019. His research spans coding theory, algorithm design, and system implementation. He previously conducted research on communication coding at Huawei and contributed to core algorithm architecture for cryptographic accelerators at an integrated-circuit design company. His experience spans theoretical modeling, algorithm optimization, and hardware/software system implementation."

[[introduction]]
content = "His current research focuses on coding theory and trusted computing. He has long studied Reed–Solomon codes and finite-field algorithms, with an emphasis on low-power, high-reliability applications such as deep-sea communications. His work addresses reliable transmission over complex channels, concatenated coding, burst-channel modeling, and efficient decoding, with the goal of jointly optimizing coding performance and computational complexity. For large-scale AI-driven sensing systems, he also explores intersections between coding theory and cryptographic techniques such as zero-knowledge proofs. This work investigates verifiable computing and trusted sensing mechanisms that protect raw-data privacy while enabling proof of computational correctness, auditable data provenance, and trustworthy system operation, with applications in intelligent sensing networks and emerging trusted infrastructure."
