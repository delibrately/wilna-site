Cooperative Escort by Multiple Submersibles

Research question
How can multiple autonomous underwater vehicles coordinate to escort a crewed submersible amid ocean currents?
Key approach
Software-defined networking for underwater IoV supports coordination, while an artificial-flow potential-field algorithm plans multi-submersible paths.
