Research / 04.04

Unmanned Ground Vehicles

Autonomous operation of unmanned ground vehicles, coordinating environmental sensing, localization, route planning, and vehicle control.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can a vehicle continuously estimate its state and surroundings on roads or in work areas?
  2. How can perception be translated into safe routes and actions?
  3. How can task execution remain stable with limited communication, compute, or sensing capability?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Perception and localization

Onboard sensors provide information about nearby objects and roads while estimating vehicle position and motion.

02

Route planning and control

Driving decisions are generated from the environment, task objectives, and safety constraints, then executed by vehicle controllers.

03

System coordination

Timing and resources are coordinated across onboard computing, communication, and motion control.
