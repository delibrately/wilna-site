Research / 03.02

On-Device Models

Deploying and running intelligent models on devices with constrained compute, memory, and energy budgets.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can models that meet task requirements be deployed within limited compute and storage?
  2. How can inference speed, resource use, and task performance be balanced after compression?
  3. How can inference adapt to device capability and input workload?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Compression and distillation

Knowledge distillation and structural pruning are approaches to reduce model size and computation.

02

On-device inference

Execution and data movement are optimized around device compute, memory, and power constraints.

03

Collaborative deployment

Model partitioning and task coordination can make use of both device and edge-node capabilities.
