Multimodal sensing

UWBeacon: Centimeter-Level Underwater Positioning

Research question
How can accurate positioning work underwater amid poor visibility and strong ambient-light interference?
Key approach
LED beacons and a camera receiver use polarized lights of different colors and angles to suppress background interference.
MobiCom 2024 ↗: Paper details — UWBeacon: Centimeter-Level Underwater Positioning

Communications & networks

AquaLink: Underwater Optical Camera Communication

Research question
How can reliable communication work in dynamic, bandwidth-limited underwater settings without specialized transceivers?
Key approach
QR-code optical camera communication on commodity phones and tablets combines blue-green encoding, LDPC error correction, and environmental adaptation.
IEEE TMC 2026 ↗: Paper details — AquaLink: Underwater Optical Camera Communication

Collaborative edge intelligence

Adaptive Task Offloading for Industrial IoT

Research question
How can latency-sensitive Industrial IoT tasks adapt quickly while reducing labeled-data and training costs?
Key approach
A semi-supervised reservoir-computing framework keeps a dynamic reservoir fixed, trains only the readout layer, and uses unlabeled data.
IEEE TMC 2026 ↗: Paper details — Adaptive Task Offloading for Industrial IoT

Physical AI systems

Cooperative Escort by Multiple Submersibles

Research question
How can multiple autonomous underwater vehicles coordinate to escort a crewed submersible amid ocean currents?
Key approach
Software-defined networking for underwater IoV supports coordination, while an artificial-flow potential-field algorithm plans multi-submersible paths.
IEEE TITS 2023 ↗: Paper details — Cooperative Escort by Multiple Submersibles
