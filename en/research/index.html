01

Acoustic Sensing and Acoustic Localization

Understanding environments and locating sound sources through audio. We explore microphone arrays, signal processing, and learning methods for event recognition and localization in noise and reverberation, with applications in smart spaces, robotics, and human-computer interaction.

Explore research ↗: Acoustic Sensing and Acoustic Localization
02

Wireless Sensing

Using wireless signals to observe people and their surroundings without contact. Channel variations and Doppler information support activity, identity, and spatial sensing. Our research considers cross-scene generalization, robustness, and real-time deployment.
