Adaptive Task Offloading for Industrial IoT

Research question
How can latency-sensitive Industrial IoT tasks adapt quickly while reducing labeled-data and training costs?
Key approach
A semi-supervised reservoir-computing framework keeps a dynamic reservoir fixed, trains only the readout layer, and uses unlabeled data.
