Research / 04.01

Underwater Robots

Sensing, localization, communication, and task execution for autonomous underwater vehicles.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can robots obtain useful environmental and teammate information with constrained underwater positioning and communication?
  2. How can multiple submersibles exchange state and coordinate routes and tasks?
  3. How can sensing, communication, and motion decisions adapt to underwater constraints?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Underwater sensing and navigation

Acoustic, optical, and inertial observations can support target localization and motion-state estimation.

02

Cooperative communication

Information transfer, network organization, and task-state sharing are considered for underwater nodes.

03

Route and task planning

Multi-submersible coordination is organized around mission objectives, formation requirements, and environmental constraints.

Related projects

Physical AI systems

Cooperative Escort by Multiple Submersibles

Research question
How can multiple autonomous underwater vehicles coordinate to escort a crewed submersible amid ocean currents?
Key approach
Software-defined networking for underwater IoV supports coordination, while an artificial-flow potential-field algorithm plans multi-submersible paths.
