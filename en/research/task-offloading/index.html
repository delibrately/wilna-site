Research / 03.01

Task Offloading

Assigning computation among devices, edge nodes, and the cloud according to deadlines, device capability, and network conditions.

Questions

Research question

  1. Where should tasks execute under changing networks and computing loads?
  2. How can latency and device energy be reduced while meeting service constraints?
  3. How can offloading adapt to changing tasks and environments with limited labeled data?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Task and resource modeling

Task computation, data size, deadlines, device capability, and communication cost inform offloading decisions.

02

Online offloading

Reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and semi-supervised methods can adapt task assignment to system state.

03

Multi-node coordination

Resources and workloads are coordinated across devices, edge servers, and network nodes.

Related projects

Collaborative edge intelligence

Adaptive Task Offloading for Industrial IoT

Research question
How can latency-sensitive Industrial IoT tasks adapt quickly while reducing labeled-data and training costs?
Key approach
A semi-supervised reservoir-computing framework keeps a dynamic reservoir fixed, trains only the readout layer, and uses unlabeled data.
