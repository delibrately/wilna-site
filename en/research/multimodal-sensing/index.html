Multimodal Sensing

Fusing sound, wireless signals, and images to sense people, devices, and environments, including complex underwater settings.

Research Goal

How can different types of information, including sound, wireless signals, and images, be fused to sense people, devices, and environments, including in complex underwater settings?

Specific Topics

  • Acoustic localization
  • Wireless sensing (including Wi-Fi sensing)

UWBeacon: Centimeter-Level Underwater Positioning

Research question
How can accurate positioning work underwater amid poor visibility and strong ambient-light interference?
Key approach
LED beacons and a camera receiver use polarized lights of different colors and angles to suppress background interference.
