Industrial Internet of Things

Device connectivity and latency-sensitive applications in industrial settings, spanning network coordination, edge computing, and task processing.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can industrial services remain reliable as devices, links, and computing loads change?
  2. How should tasks be assigned among devices, edge nodes, and the cloud?
  3. How can real-time response, resource use, and communication reliability be balanced?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Industrial connectivity

Heterogeneous devices, data flows, service requirements, and network constraints shape the system design.

02

Collaborative edge computing

Task offloading, resource allocation, and edge-node coordination can shorten processing paths.

03

Adaptive services

Online status and historical information can guide task processing under latency and reliability constraints.

Related projects

Collaborative edge intelligence

Adaptive Task Offloading for Industrial IoT

Research question
How can latency-sensitive Industrial IoT tasks adapt quickly while reducing labeled-data and training costs?
Key approach
A semi-supervised reservoir-computing framework keeps a dynamic reservoir fixed, trains only the readout layer, and uses unlabeled data.
