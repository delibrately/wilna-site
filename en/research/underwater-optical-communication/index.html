Underwater Optical Communication

Using underwater visible light and cameras to transmit information, with attention to link reliability and practical deployment in dynamic water.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can an optical link remain usable under scattering, occlusion, and device motion?
  2. Can commodity cameras and displays reduce reliance on specialized transceivers?
  3. How should throughput, range, reliability, and device resources be balanced?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Optical link design

The work considers visible-light propagation, blue-green wavelengths, and camera imaging characteristics.

02

Encoding and error correction

Image encoding, QR-code payloads, and error-correction mechanisms can improve tolerance to bit errors and image distortion.

03

Environmental adaptation

Water turbidity, distance, motion, and ambient light are considered in receiver-quality adaptation.
