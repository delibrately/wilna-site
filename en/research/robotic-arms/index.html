Research / 04.02

Robotic Arms

Calibrating the spatial relationship between robotic arms and vision sensors as a foundation for vision-guided manipulation.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can the transformation between camera and robot coordinate frames be estimated accurately?
  2. How can joint calibration account for different errors in robot measurements and visual observations?
  3. How do calibration errors affect localization, grasping, and manipulation?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Hand-eye calibration

Robot motion and visual observations establish the geometric relationship between a camera and the end effector.

02

Robot-world joint calibration

Transform parameters among the robot, vision sensor, and reference frame are estimated jointly.

03

Accuracy analysis and optimization

The effects of measurement errors on parameter estimation inform calibration optimization.
