Research / 01.03

Underwater Sensing

Combining acoustic, optical, and wireless signals for underwater positioning and environmental awareness.

Questions

Research question

  1. How can underwater observations remain reliable amid low visibility, scattering, and complex noise?
  2. How can acoustic, optical, and wireless measurements be fused for positioning and scene understanding?
  3. How can underwater sensing support downstream tasks for robots and sensor networks?

Approach

Technical approach

01

Multimodal acquisition

Underwater cameras, optical beacons, acoustic devices, and wireless sensor nodes provide observations suited to each setting.

02

Positioning and fusion

Image, signal-strength, and time-of-arrival features can be combined while accounting for uncertainty.

03

Adaptation to underwater conditions

The work considers how turbidity, ambient light, scattering, and device differences affect sensing reliability.
