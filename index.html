We are a research lab of Wilna, led by Prof. Lei Wang.

Our lab focuses on wireless sensing, acoustic sensing, acoustic localization, underwater networking, and intelligent sensing systems.

Undergraduate students who are highly self-motivated and interested in these research directions are welcome to join Wilna lab. Contact: lei.wang@dlut.edu.cn
